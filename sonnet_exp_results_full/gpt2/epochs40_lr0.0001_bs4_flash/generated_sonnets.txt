Two loves I have, of comfort and despair,
That like two spirits do suggest me still.
My better angel is a man right fair,
And love that endures well proves me dead.
From him my evil omens do I flatter,
And to him my good cheer doth lie.
For if it err, not from me, it no longer flies,
And I am sprung from the same root,
Like two fools who have sworn their lives in fair.
But if it err, not from me, it no longer flies,
And in my madness, I am cast forth.
  For to him my good name keeps fast my soul,
  And evil's hold on me, now alive and bold.
