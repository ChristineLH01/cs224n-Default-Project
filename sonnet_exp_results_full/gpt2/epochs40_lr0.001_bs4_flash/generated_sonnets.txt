Those lips that Love's own hand did make
Breathed forth the sound that said "I hate"
To me that languished for her sake;
That she hath thee is of my woe chief,
A loss in love that touches me more nearly.
Loving offenders, thus I will excuse ye:
Thou dost love her because thou know'st I love her,
And for my sake even so doth she abuse me,
Suff'ring my friend for my sake to approve her.
If I lose thee, my loss is my love's gain,
And losing her, my friend hath found that loss;
Both find each other, and I lose both twain,
And both for my sake lay on me this cross.

Poor soul, the center of my sinful earth,
Pressed with these rebel powers that thee array,
Why dost thou pine within and suffer dearth,
While your outer shell shines with such brilliance?
My eye, wide as it is, doth plea,
"Truth needs no grave for his love;
Beauty needs no graces, for it is the best;
When combined, they lose their pure, individual pull?"
Because he needs no flattery, will you stay mute?
Silence is not pardon, for it lies within you
To grant him life beyond a golden crypt,
And praise him through the ages yet to debut.
  Now, perform your duty, muse; I show you how
  To make him live as he does

My love is as a fever, longing still
For that which longer nurseth the disease,
Feeding on that which doth preserve the ill,
And is partly blind, but effectually is
Doth but blind, as the blind do attest.
For if it err I not from those lips that lie,
Adorned in red, they've sealed false bonds ere now.
My eyes, once full of your unmatched deep,
Are moored within a harbor of the mind;
Yet, Love, you've shaped deceitful manacles,
That bind my heart to follow where they're blind.
  If I should lose thee, my heart's history,
  In love's miss, my heart would say,
  The doom is truth


3
O me, what eyes hath love put in my head,
Which have no correspondence with true sight!
Or if they have, where is my judgment fled,
Why then, fearful of time's tyranny?
If I cannot write to you, nor truly sympathized
The well that leads me to go,
My loss is my love's gain,
And that, in turn, shall bring me to you.
O, lest the world should task you to recite
What merit lived in me that you should love,
After my death, dear love, forget me quite,
For you in me can nothing worthy prove;
  O, lest your heart should look into sleep,
  Your true love, in my mind, is spent.

6
Love is too young to know what conscience is;
Yet who knows not conscience is born of love?
Then, gentle cheater, urge not my amiss,
Lest you become entangled in my errors.
For, as you deceive me, I forsake
My nobler self to my body's treachery.
My soul whispers to my flesh that it may soar
In love; the flesh, no longer a bore,
Rises at your name, its power to explore,
A triumphant trophy, yours to adore.
  And thus, in pride, it serves, always near,
  To rise and fall, for your love, my dear.

And, tender churl, mak'st waste in thought,

If from thyself to thee I be deceived.

Calls back the lovely April of her prime;

9
The little love-god, lying once asleep,
Laid by his side his heart-inflaming brand,
Whilst many nymphs that vowed chaste life to keep alive,
With virtuous hand and foot, they granted him reprieve,
And left him unscathed, to languish, devoid of many a lover's glare.
The morning sun, in its heavenly course,
Nor the bright star that heralds twilight's hour,
Can match the solemn beauty of your glance,
Bestowing grace on the fire,
Yet, lacking your memory, he lingered on the trail,
Lost to the world, for which you are sworn.
  So I, in love's dread, are being at a house,
  To one who shines in your warm blaze.


10
When my love swears that she is made of truth,
I do believe her, though I know she lies,
That she might think me some untutored youth,
And in dreams, a thing removed was my frame;
How could I, in dreams, perceive the world's change,
And in their union, every part
Of me, they thought it but a part,
A poet's tale, no more, they say much more.
How could I, in my mind, deceive one another in my act,
To cherish life, in which I am confined?
  All my grief is my legacy, and my fire,
  Together, we traverse the endless tide.
