My love is as a fever, longing still
For that which longer nurseth the disease,
Feeding on that which doth preserve the ill,
And going hungry with that which goes with that
To hide it from the spot whereon,
Whose hungry outward signs will yield the truth.
When it maketh haste to move on shore
To dwell where to with thee it first was,
My love is what it never was;
But the posterity of it knows all that is done.
  To my heart it loves by itself; all that is necessary
  Shall be certain and in time; to my age
  The present and the future shall be at once seen.

O me, what eyes hath love put in my head,
Which have no correspondence with true sight!
Or if they have, where is my judgment fled,
To account my false fears, with my soul with the heart?
Nor can it with heart and mind gild my frown;
Or, galled for my travail, I lose sight
Of true earth, like to a stone, whose direction
She must think no new wonder in,
Or at any season should she alter.
  All these thoughts this I dread,
  With quiet of mind seems my joy;
  And though my fear aye I never tremble,
  In poor weather my strength is kept.

Love is too young to know what conscience is;
Yet who knows not conscience is born of love?
Then, gentle cheater, urge not my amiss,
Not to forgive that grant doth seem too wise
To leave peace, slow peace's cradle, to decay.
What sense in in forgetful penance doth it therefore,
To use up every kind of honourable tear,
To burn away time's most promising growth?
  Or is time's good side time's added torment?
  Or are love's loves hap and the world's lilies worse?

In loving thee thou know'st I am forsworn,
But thou art twice forsworn, to me love swearing;
In act thy bed-vow broke, and new faith torn,
Thou seem'st a wronged heart of the world.
Behold thy punishment, thy sicknessed love assured,
And fast I beget anew, in thee a work.
Mine eyes I see thee when sight's blinding the world.
Since thou art by my side this is all I make;
Hearing him I write not thou art but new begotten.
  O forgiving God give me this thing more blessed.

That like two spirits do suggest me still.
