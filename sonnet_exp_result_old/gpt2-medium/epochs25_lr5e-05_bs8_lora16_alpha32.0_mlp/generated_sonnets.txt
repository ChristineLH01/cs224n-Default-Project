Cupid laid by his brand and fell asleep.
A maid of Dian's this advantage found,
And his love-kindling fire did quickly steep.
Happy abode in stars he soon deserved,
Now sunes on an afterwards mad then his changes,
And found forgotten dower in the dust made.
His widow warned, but starved not
That he left noble care of himself undispotted,
And when careless he received, being feasted
By idols most great when absent.

Two loves I have, of comfort and despair,
That like two spirits do suggest me still.
My better angel is a man right fair,
Who to laugh brings forth pleasant health;
Nor to grieving make sounds of glad grief.
But left me what may bear, none but these bears.
I my prettiness I forget
Then give mind with ought and such to lose it
As young dogs without shoes make top hats,
Every bird be feigned supposed a peasant.
  None such to desire barbarity could be.
  So yet worthy may be the memory of grief,
