When my love swears that she is made of truth,
I do believe her, though I know she lies,

Who for joy unlooked upon lies at secret count.

Two loves I have, of comfort and despair,
That like two spirits do suggest me still.
My better angel is a man right fair,
Among wizards rich, and blessed sire,
Full free from sight or glance that eaveswore,
So strong as strong for dry and lasting fear.
Then arise two damned thoughts, good my love for object,
Those discontents wherein I root
And two prisoners of my sorrows house.
One another can consider no unfairness,
But terror evil can through scorned vile prostrate;
One else is seldom taken for terror,
But joy for total desired satisfaction.
  On equal laws hold the beauty and fear.
