Two loves I have, of comfort and despair,
That like two spirits do suggest me still.
My better angel is a man right fair,
And in his might I find my sweet rest;
The lesser loves are but sweet imitations of his,
And tenders to his pleasure, as he fits them.
My evil muse doth sing of thee, and doth write
As if thee were the star, shining so bright;
But, bright as heaven, she hath but slight art,
A poet's fair present, less false than wild.
  In our two loves there lives but one difference,
  That we two bitterly hate one another.
