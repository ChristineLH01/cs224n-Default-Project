Those lips that Love's own hand did make
Breathed forth the sound that said "I hate"
To me that languished for her sake;
They could neglect nothing of love, it bore,
Waste nothing of gain from his self-same she:
Even now, if their hearts believed nor hated aught,
They might not; it lived with pleasure that fed
Not from love the hunger of desire.
Love ragged from the poor pump'd earth earns
Kind circles fresh, water, sunshine watered every day.
  Until love you so loved robbed lives,
  Neither yours, nor misattributed other can be bought.

When my love swears that she is made of truth,
I do believe her, though I know she lies,
That she might think me some untutored youth,
Most fit for a false satire.
But the poets oft were poor & replete with lies
Since thou shouldst sing to refresh me
When they do gain contented display
With their lies undusted; your verses
O'er-cost me much where you build a rich cave.
I know the verse well that smiles thou lov'st most
And not gaudy words that defend cruel smiles.
  But mine is the least girlish strength, & so

Two loves I have, of comfort and despair,
That like two spirits do suggest me still.
My better angel is a man right fair,
Who glad doth make window spring light,
Hate buttress of robbers' scythe,
Haters root out timid plants, root up crooked shape;
Who runs wail, and shall heal out of undone.
All their date doth live for glory!
Hard to reform from hidden loopholes shall live
When five slices of praise lie pointed to thee.
For out of romance should love excite his rage,
Sighting for him till he cuts off devotion.
  When noon is out the 'veil'd eyes write quite bold
  From wicked throes to holy beauty lay.
