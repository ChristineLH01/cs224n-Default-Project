When my love swears that she is made of truth,
I do believe her, though I know she lies,
That she might think me some untutored youth,
Had she but untruthless eyes put into spades.
Good trees bear green leaves, nothing cover them down,
And the fair woods seldom close;
All the depths dwell within, sorrows all around
Because he deceived his light there.
That red leaves never lie buried nor weakened,
All of which, though their heat stand on five to one,
And those still lie doth every change revolve;
There she, beauteous and fat, makes me so stupid
That I give thee sweet shade when it lies hideless in the noon.
