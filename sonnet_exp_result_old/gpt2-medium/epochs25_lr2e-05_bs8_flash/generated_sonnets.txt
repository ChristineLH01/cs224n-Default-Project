Love is too young to know what conscience is;
Yet who knows not conscience is born of love?
Then, gentle cheater, urge not my amiss,
As heartless love seeks to steal what is owed.
Pause, weigh love's full weight; don't waste
My time, which could be spent on you.
Let my love be admired, not reproachy,
For it knows no bounds, and never will.
Pause; in reflection, love knows
No double privilege, bliss dwells in naught.
Pause, observe how two hearts in love
In harmony, create a cosmos
  To love one another above all things.

In loving thee thou know'st I am forsworn,
But thou art twice forsworn, to me love swearing;
In act thy bed-vow broke, and new faith torn,
Save for that old faith of mine before thou sworn;
Therefore there arose this thought in my mind,
To see why thy vows this good year
Sang but ill pleas, being other's praised;
And to preposterousness sent along
The heavens of capriciousness ruling interweave,
Disputed prove must I outlast, with worlds restored,
Both my intent and course being called former.
  Thus wasting motion my satire sore aches,
  To frame presbyter false praises come to life.

Two loves I have, of comfort and despair,
That like two spirits do suggest me still.
My better angel is a man right fair,
And poor lover hates to dwell near.
A heart so full of hope, it gives me respite,
But love's sting is in my dark abyss.
Oh, what a cure for love's dark disgrace,
To find a ray of hope in my dove!
But my better devil doth well array
The pearls of tears, that my heart doth craved.
  So, well-balanced, happy man or unhappy wife,
  Both fed by love's own fire, or else lost in despair.
