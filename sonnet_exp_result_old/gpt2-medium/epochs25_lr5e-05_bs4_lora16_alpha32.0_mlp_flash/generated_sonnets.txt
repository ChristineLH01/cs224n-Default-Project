Two loves I have, of comfort and despair,
That like two spirits do suggest me still.
My better angel is a man right fair,
Which likes a society affectionate;
But for worldly honor lets his figure do show,
Requisite every appearance of beauty,
For he neither worships humbleness nor separates.
  And one of my best treasure is divers fancies,
  The theft of mine own eyes into known kinds;
  So loose were mine erstwhile eyes, so safe is this book;
  That kisses of praise for false incantation,
  Let passers to their faces hear.
