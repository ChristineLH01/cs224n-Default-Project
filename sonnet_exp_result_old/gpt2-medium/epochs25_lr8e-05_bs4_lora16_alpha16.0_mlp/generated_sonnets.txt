When my love swears that she is made of truth,
I do believe her, though I know she lies,
That she might think me some untutored youth,
Like beast hunted awake among sleeping folk;
For whose blood if warm she bears the sod,
Or which mayst tread assails to the sewers.
Thou wouldst avoid tragedy by caution;
For I ought not eke dangers into spoils alone.
So do I look on all sorrow and hope
From heaven, having been won by thy wailing grace;
Having found cheer and returned debarred fall,
Ruled disheartened which things don and ruled flatter.
