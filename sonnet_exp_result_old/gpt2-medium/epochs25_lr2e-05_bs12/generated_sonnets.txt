Poor soul, the center of my sinful earth,
Pressed with these rebel powers that thee array,
Why dost thou pine within and suffer dearth,
When thou do'st subsist and leave no evidence?
Grant me the strength to pray for thy blissful state,
Anon my lines begin to fray, and flow in twain,
Bound by the chaotic, hollow urge to drain.
Erase them from remembrance, forget them not,
Or at least be the first to know they're gone,
Find healing within thy blighted core,
Obscured as they are by thee and all that's holy.
  Take pity on this broken vessel, let her see
  The light of day, though it may miss her at her height.

For that which longer nurseth the disease,

  This wretched creature, by your side, keeps thee warm.
