To me that languished for her sake;

Of what I esteemed as mine own due;

Poor soul, the center of my sinful earth,
Pressed with these rebel powers that thee array,
Why dost thou pine within and suffer dearth,
When every private can have his voice?
Send to Syracuse my loyal subjects and have them fight
To the death, each by the other's hand defeated;
And having compiled a better state,
Look to the cloistered blessings of thy isolation,
And praise it through the endless night.
  Yet, love, persist not. This war is for thee and for me

Two loves I have, of comfort and despair,
That like two spirits do suggest me still.
My better angel is a man right fair,
And in his aspect stands the counterfeit of my shame;
His shadow is my solace, and mine eye
Bliss when I think of thee.
The counterfeit of his grace is this:
My heart is thine, his beauty my defect,
And in these defects lies my doom.
So then, I will live, like two spirits in love,
Who one day lose and then find new,
Two souls entwined in harmony and peace.
  Or thus I'll say:
  Two loves entwined in harmony and peace make one.
