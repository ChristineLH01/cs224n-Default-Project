Two loves I have, of comfort and despair,
That like two spirits do suggest me still.
My better angel is a man right fair,
At right posted in the old wonder;
But of him I live, and should all transient have me
Pass away this revery, unused;
For looking to life's early absence in ruin,
My unlucky position shall stay for many an age.
  My begetter a wife more cunning,
  And love whose true sweetness can love mine feare.
