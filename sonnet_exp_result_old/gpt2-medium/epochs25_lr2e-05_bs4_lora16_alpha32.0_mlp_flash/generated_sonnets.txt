Poor soul, the center of my sinful earth,
Pressed with these rebel powers that thee array,
Why dost thou pine within and suffer dearth,
Abates and showers treasures thine own tender seams;
Seems indifferent reed, pitying the gift and anxious to borrow;
But remember thyself, and that thorn toward season worsted.
Mine are tempest, not storms that chide chastity,
What attend their ominous foiling meet
That doesn't cut thy soul to essence.
  O, good pardon me! innocent girl is this!
  For deepest truest love is thou suffused in:
  Well thou art far from two other beauties.

My love is as a fever, longing still
For that which longer nurseth the disease,
Feeding on that which doth preserve the ill,
Burning away evil attributes 'gainst us and for and when might preserve
My own glorified thee and plain truth
For incomparable being; being beautified, yet standest;
For perfect nature is whored abroad,
And therefore love lusts; and so find I in thee,
Whilst in inwardmost minds, holy young fires' daughter,
Three made for a nest is undying,
Refreshed thereby as sweet-fire long woe defends.
  And, I swear, verily like to never rest
  When that may stay my lonely youthful fires to keep me.

8
Cupid laid by his brand and fell asleep.
A maid of Dian's this advantage found,
And his love-kindling fire did quickly steep;
"Grow this slow fire then sweet moss may fast,
And water slow streams do flow with that aid."
Then methinks the poison, his vein's cure,
That found tongues ague might domesticate.
So their honey grew like blood from stone unto stone.
Then he that reeked knew kings gone,
Gazelles of prison and service bought,
Now with tears purged healing rests,
Head steeped against his false man--the perfect warrior.
  His favour I for shame love granted still,
  Time rents third king with a fourth personal.

The little love-god, lying once asleep,
Laid by his side his heart-inflaming brand,
Whilst many nymphs that vowed chaste life to keep
The spirit of lust still abound'd in their hazel'nut orbs,
As diviners among clerks of hearts found him;
Over their counterfeits rose a luxury to lavish
Above every employment incommoded,
And deem that lover's modesty could keep
By distinction in rhetoric or sentence won,
