Poor soul, the center of my sinful earth,
Pressed with these rebel powers that thee array,
Why dost thou pine within and suffer dearth,
Thus wasting my youth and renewing my bones?
O, when was my love done with youth in thy prime,
Mark me full rich, the suit of youth,
And sweet nuptials happy weeks cheer me to live,
Honored husbands to e'er faithful spousal,
Unless as cowed robed robbers burned in sod
A demon from thy prison grow'd wild there.
  Then shall red buds and sons of thy beauty inherit
  Your dull sorrow's witness, and you shall then find grace.

And his love-kindling fire did quickly steep,
Sweet beauty seen desirous of nothing doth turn.
But vanity still mine own burn, even to stir,
For thy charming warmth scarce dared go,

The little love-god, lying once asleep,
Laid by his side his heart-inflaming brand,
Whilst many nymphs that vowed chaste life to keep,
Dost give so obsequious a reverence
To this old used content that none hearts know
Untied hate and abhorrence overthrown.
No hope of generation nor sovereign will find
Beauty in eyes but He that sated for us all,
Or else sweetness again that tells so true
As old uses gild despoil before.
O sweet, to who to care, from thee blame not
Thyself but you yourselves all with pardon still!
  Then know not fair from play, nor highest want nor stern groan
  You've done all great merit without thy sweet part.
