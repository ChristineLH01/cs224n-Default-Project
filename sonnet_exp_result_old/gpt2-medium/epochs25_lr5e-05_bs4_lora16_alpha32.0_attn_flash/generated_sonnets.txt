The little love-god, lying once asleep,
Laid by his side his heart-inflaming brand,
Whilst many nymphs that vowed chaste life to keep
Shook the lip fearing their own absent love
From sweet wreaths of moss that often wove resbound hearts.
Since a harmless beauty once fell mischievous
With his frivolous gorge bound with lament anguish,
Thus did misfortune drive him henceforth,
And suck dry far hope of people to bear him dear.
Chronoth can only cry and linger dumb.
   Love so unprovided must prey eternal
  Of subtle horrors whom shame divine keeps hid.

When my love swears that she is made of truth,
I do believe her, though I know she lies,

For ambitious for justice and on the truest art
Would not bear preferring human descent:
See, God's infeal hath said in me reason;
He doth argue with writing fine proofs;
And in reminding of this treat make
In pencil I write the teaching most rather.
Even thus an analysis do I dost think
And my considerableness allures—goddess extort;
Thus flattery casting for correction within my main.
  In point of thought I am not proved, but more,
  Knowing what nearly taught seems pencilled into gold.
