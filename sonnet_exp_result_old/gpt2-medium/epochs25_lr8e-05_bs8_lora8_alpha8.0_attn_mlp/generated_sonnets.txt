My love is as a fever, longing still
For that which longer nurseth the disease,
Feeding on that which doth preserve the ill,
For that which doth him justice exercise:
These, to keep the sorrow from ending,
Offerth without bars to starvation;
For to conquer these never, morrow, gain,
But sparing gratis grows me deep hunger.
Let my love prolong burn within all nature,
For its fruit sown with mine intense
Behaves unhindered heaven, O Life, that infuse itself with my joy.
