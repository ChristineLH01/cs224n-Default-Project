Feeding on that which doth preserve the ill,

Two loves I have, of comfort and despair,
That like two spirits do suggest me still.
My better angel is a man right fair,
And my darker phantom is a dove.
Two souls in one, yet twain,
Like shadows, they move in opposite directions.
My fair one loves man, my darker phantom bears
A bond to thee, a deceitful art.
They dance, one on the dance floor,
Alternating places, with malice kindled.
  My fair one whispers softly,
  In love's sweet thoughts, his evil deeds sworn.
