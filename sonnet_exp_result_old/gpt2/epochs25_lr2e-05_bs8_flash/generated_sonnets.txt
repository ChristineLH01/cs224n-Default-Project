Poor soul, the center of my sinful earth,
Pressed with these rebel powers that thee array,
Why dost thou pine within and suffer dearth,
With vain and false inspirations lend and shun
The teeming waters of healing strength,
Thy very substance binds but for your departure?
But, Mars, love turns away the iron bars,
And gives you all strength and force
To transform Nature's excellent policy,
A fortress laid on every rack,
Where you, my dearest, shall claim dominion over thee,
Time's sovereign, still retains not.
  Blessed be the benevolent deity that she is,
  Praising your virtue when she shatters hearts,
  Lest evil's unholy designs unfurl.
