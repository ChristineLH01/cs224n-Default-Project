Those lips that Love's own hand did make
Breathed forth the sound that said "I hate"
To me that languished for her sake;
Her lips, which summer's touch brought forth,
A mirror image of their own corruption;
Tears would have told the lie, taunted lips,
And whispered not "I love you."
But I, denied love, should not endure,
For I loved only with hearts as such,
And not with souls as such, who bleed as if
Adorning balm with darkness red.
  To live with one was to shun sorrow,
  And live with two, to shun the blight.

Cupid laid by his brand and fell asleep.
A maid of Dian's this advantage found,
And his love-kindling fire did quickly steepen.
The morning sun, in its heavenly course, did chase
A raven soaring in the skies;
But, no longer chased by mortals' gaze,
Myon Love's flame rekindled in the night,
A gentle, healing light to all men's hearts.
Myon, beaming from the temple of his love,
As if by a magician bound, did summon,
A spirit of youth in him, as the lonely sneaker;
In this glow, I learned the truth of worth,
  And soon found myon Love's true name, Love's Cup.

Two loves I have, of comfort and despair,
That like two spirits do suggest me still.
My better angel is a man right fair,
And by his beauty seeming false he deceases me quite;
How can false love be so vile in this wicked humor?

When falsehood sings and speaks of us?

Of well-matched voices can be stained,
How can false choir endure song,
