The little love-god, lying once asleep,
Laid by his side his heart-inflaming brand,
Whilst many nymphs that vowed chaste life to keep,
Came upon him to offer reverence, in their silence removed,
Their fervent plea to his cold, cold, disdain.
Tears for love grieve the traitor, the glutton,
For pity's sweet passion is cold, venomous,
Though it may stain the tongue; if tasted, is eternal to us,
Which yet attains its lofty self, near our doom.
  For love bleeds in desolation, eternally ignominious,
  While bitterness steals the mark of remorse.

When my love swears that she is made of truth,
I do believe her, though I know she lies,
That she might think me some untutored youth,
That I defy uncertain innovation, hide
Her true locks beneath their bars.
Then truth makes her queen, though age knows no age,
And in darkness has her mistress unfertilized,
Rejecting the role of truthifice.
Alas, no lover stays married, for love,
In allusion, she makes us both make lovers!
  She bestows her charm on us; a fool, she knows,
  Could never soothe the lovers' hearts there.
