Two loves I have, of comfort and despair,
That like two spirits do suggest me still.
My better angel is a man right fair,
And love's fair thou dearer still.
A steadfast beacon in the heart's vast ocean,
Unwavering faithfulness proved
To steer my course towards the haven I call home.
But thou, whose form so gracious, takes
My buoyancy, and I am left in despair.
Thou cannot claim my love alone,
For love's sweet power, so replete,
Shall but enhance my dearest friend, held in light.
  Thus, I depart, and with them rejoin,
