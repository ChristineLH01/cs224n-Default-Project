Two loves I have, of comfort and despair,
That like two spirits do suggest me still.
My better angel is a man right fair,
No mortal blindness or scornful rash to red,
And, too free, saith my degraded mind,
Which wild lust on its queen's flag forsakes.
A man may try, no body unwilling,
Which an indestructible be sedately saith,
To edification or valorous story, to boasting
His pageant there. So cannot him be crooked,
As child of war, since damnation is an indigent;
  But I hate him whose pre-crown stages reign,
