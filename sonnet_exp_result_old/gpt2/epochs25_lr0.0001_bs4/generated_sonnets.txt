Pressed with these rebel powers that thee array,
Why dost thou pine within and suffer dearth,
Denying the beacon of the day to stray?
Why dost thou forsake the lowly worm,

  Pity the gentlest, therefore, and know this truth:
  Your love is as a fever, if ever.
