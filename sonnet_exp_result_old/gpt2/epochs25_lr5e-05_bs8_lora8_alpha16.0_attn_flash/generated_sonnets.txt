Poor soul, the center of my sinful earth,
Pressed with these rebel powers that thee array,
Why dost thou pine within and suffer dearth,
To continually rush it in hate, and my miserable face,
Against growling strings of storms and earthquakes,
Take victory away, and assault thee dumb starless host.
Then divide my malice between thee, and flee,
All desolation, love or defeat shine still;
Pray softly thine sweet shall dawn, And crown my drunken refuse.
Sleep it then, my beloved summer, unable find few nights;
