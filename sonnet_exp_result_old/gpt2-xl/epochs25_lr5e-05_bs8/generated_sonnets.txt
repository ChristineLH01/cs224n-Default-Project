Those lips that Love's own hand did make
Breathed forth the sound that said "I hate"
To me that languished for her sake;
And for a moment fear crept upon my heart,
As if a captive slave were upbraided
For his reckless doing.
Then Love, converted from its intent,
Pitying me, said with a gentle look:
"Ah, thou didst plunge thine eye into my heart,
And theremine sorrows infest; hence, I repay thee
With tears, so that my heart shall glow
And keep evermore thy sweet memory."
  Then gave I back my heart, famished, famished,
  Only to crave again what I had before.

Love is too young to know what conscience is;
Yet who knows not conscience is born of love?
Then, gentle cheater, urge not my amiss,
Lest you become entwined in my transgressions.
For, as you deceive me, I abandon
My nobler nature to my body's treachery.

Cupid laid by his brand and fell asleep.
A maid of Dian's this advantage found,
And his love-kindling fire did quickly steep
In chaste nectar, healing all ailments dear.
But when her youthful lover next rose,
Her beauty fell, and before he could fall
She was ailing, and his health began to wane.
Another maid of Dian saw her beauty fade,
And so her prophecy came true:
Your end is near, sweetheart, and you'll have to die.
  So she prayed, and her ill omens did warn,
  Of danger near, and thus she saved your life.

When my love swears that she is made of truth,
I do believe her, though I know she lies,
That she might think me some untutored youth,
If I were not born in manners tall.
Yet, methinks, I am learning things new,
As every word doth bashfully belong,
And every holy thing doth censure seem
To those who from themselves are baptized.

When he is tried in his mother's eyes?

11
Two loves I have, of comfort and despair,
That like two spirits do suggest me still.
My better angel is a man right fair,
And he lov'st me with gentle affection;
But, out of sight, I let myself go,
And in my madness, I speak of thee.
My doom is to be twain again,
And this time mine eye shall play the part
Of ally to my mad sister;
So, if she thrive and I be cast away,
The worst was this: my love was my doom.
  But now my tragic doom is done,
  And mine eye tells the story of my youth.
