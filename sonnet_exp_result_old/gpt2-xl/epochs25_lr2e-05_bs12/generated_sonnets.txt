My love is as a fever, longing still
For that which longer nurseth the disease,
Feeding on that which doth preserve the ill,
Embracing the chicery wall of bitterness.
My love is as the worm that sinneth, growing stronger
And fatter with the hunger of unchastened life,
Consuming the choicest weeds of the flock,
Too harsh and cruel for the sweet love they provoke.
More blessed are they that bear their love unperturbed,
Preserving their good name, proud and straight,
Aside from the jaws of anguish, fearing no hook.
  My love is as the queen's spleen, fatigued
  Only to preach the love that is patient and bold.
