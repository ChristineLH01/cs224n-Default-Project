Poor soul, the center of my sinful earth,
Pressed with these rebel powers that thee array,
Why dost thou pine within and suffer dearth,
While thy outer bounds are ever expanded?
Why invest so much in this temporal dwelling,
A structure bound to crumble, fade, and fall?

Let them wither while you amass treasures fair.

Two loves I have, of comfort and despair,
That like two spirits do suggest me still.
My better angel is a man right fair,
And like him loves with moods and hours changed.
The darker love is that with which I am encumbered,
Which dulls my brain and makes me weep.
When these dimmed lights are unfurled,
I see monstrosities rise up to prominence,
Like weeds after the sun's anointment.
My true love's appearance is like that of day,
Dark in the violet night,
Still green when the violet falls away.
  So I swing back and forth, both sated,
  By day, from darkness to light, in quest of thee.
