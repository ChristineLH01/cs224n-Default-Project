And his love-kindling fire did quickly steep.

Than natural warmth could have bred.

And did not charge what equal spite gave.
So womenfolk, beguiled by beauty's allure,
Came to see what was wrong with me, and found
Their prince, for they saw with prescience
My heart's truth, wherein love was dressed.
  Yet, from this fall, I learned and understood:
  Your "cure" is poison, causing pain and shame.

That like two spirits do suggest me still.
