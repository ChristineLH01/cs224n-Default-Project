Two loves I have, of comfort and despair,
That like two spirits do suggest me still.
My better angel is a man right fair,
Though not so bright as my heavy heart;
Another love, less precious yet still like my heart,
Sets me too happy, like his subdued song.
Yet these sounds do not wholly embrace me,
Another love having hold of my heart,
Making sad and sadder music there.
Like two widowed hearths, if one heart do burn,
The other two shall follow my example,
In this love, I find sorrow and woe,
These echoes following my heart's encounter.
  Yet others may their discordants play,
  I in this union preserve the two.
