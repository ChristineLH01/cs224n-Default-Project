Those lips that Love's own hand did make
Breathed forth the sound that said "I hate"
To me that languished for her sake;
To me she gave no sign, no choice,
She only set a form upon my woe,
And forced my love to answer, "I choose."
This denial moved me yet more,
And thus my verse began:
"I hate" to thee, her cold abstraction,
"I choose" to thee, my soul's tumultuous ado;
In this ecstacy of rejection, I find
My heart's battle, a fight to the finish.
  "I hate" hath made her choice,
  "I choose" to thee, my heart's own vindication.

My love is as a fever, longing still
For that which longer nurseth the disease,
Feeding on that which doth preserve the ill,
Haply becoming the very thing it raves.
I have not the strength to resist,
The wretched 'twixt love and will
Is so attainted with vice, it cannot help but wane,
Or at least lose the believ'd love of will,
Which in its corruption doth impart
A sweet but hidden danger to its bed.
  Lo, thus, I've surrendered all my might,
  To one whose heart is as black as pitch-dark slate.

O me, what eyes hath love put in my head,
Which have no correspondence with true sight!

That lie in heaven's eye and in men's deceit?

Love is too young to know what conscience is;
Yet who knows not conscience is born of love?
Then, gentle cheater, urge not my amiss,
Lest I become entangled in your foison.

In loving thee thou know'st I am forsworn,
But thou art twice forsworn, to me love swearing;
In act thy bed-vow broke, and new faith torn,
And twice missed the thing that gave thee thee;
In trusting thou didst broken faith provoke,
And accomplice usurer treason.
For to my treason I pen witness,
And to thy fair fraud I bring mine injury;
And yet by self-example learning to be
My life hath in myself subdued my sense,
And confined it within thine annals.
  But yet thou art become a tyrant, a cruel master,
  To thrice bewail my sins and yet me sick.

Cupid laid by his brand and fell asleep.
A maid of Dian's this advantage found,
And his love-kindling fire did quickly steep.
She quenched the flame with a song of naught,
But when Love's fiery core met the cool,
Her expression showed the same warming glow.
A cure for lovers' pains, Love's ephemeral grace,
She secured with a crystal key,
A sacred record, Love's alchemy still practiced.
Thus, through her music, Love's essence thrived,
Despite the restraints of time and space.
  Her silence, though obligatory, Love's crypt,
  Could not imitate his dance, nor suffer under his glare.
