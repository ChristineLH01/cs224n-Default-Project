Those lips that Love's own hand did make
Breathed forth the sound that said "I hate"
To me that languished for her sake;
Those red lines that crept on my skin
To show me a perpetual friend.
Those lines, though initially designed for her,
Brought forth by her own touch, revealed
Her own strength, and stripped me of
All I ever sought, or thought I should possess.
Yet, from those lines, I learned and understood:
Her heart is boundless, vast and wide,
Will you not grant me a place to sleep?
  Her love, however vast, she does not divide,
  And yet, I, in my solitude, do take her part.

For that which longer nurseth the disease,
Feeding on that which doth preserve the ill,

Love is too young to know what conscience is;
Yet who knows not conscience is born of love?
Then, gentle cheater, urge not my amiss,
Lest you become entangled in my transgressions.
For, as you deceive me, I betray
My nobler self to my body's treachery.
My soul informs my flesh that it may strive
For victory in love; the flesh, now alive,
Rises at your name, its purpose to derive
From your affection, its reason to thrive.
  And thus, in pride, it serves, ever so near,
  To stand by you, to fall by your side, my dear.
