When my love swears that she is made of truth,
I do believe her, though I know she lies,
That she might think me some untutored youth,
Unschooled in life's ways, once she's won my heart.
Thus, I am often left in the dark,
While she pursues what seems the best,
Unmoved by the shame that binds her heart.
I hope her true love may one day shine,
While I, in my weakness, remain mute,
But still, I hope, in the end, she'll win my trust,
And know what true love is all about.
  In hope, I stay mute, though her truth is near,
  For I fear, if she knows, she'll turn away.

Two loves I have, of comfort and despair,
That like two spirits do suggest me still.
My better angel is a man right fair,
With compassion and insights high,
Yet fears my ill luck may compare.
My darker soul seeks to rob my light,
Seeking shadows like a raven in the skies.
My loves, in conflict, coax me to stay,
In hopes of a better day, kinder skies.
Yet, both say my time is done,
And side with the darkness, losing me.
  So I swing back and forth, both seesaws,
  Both sides arguing for my peace.
