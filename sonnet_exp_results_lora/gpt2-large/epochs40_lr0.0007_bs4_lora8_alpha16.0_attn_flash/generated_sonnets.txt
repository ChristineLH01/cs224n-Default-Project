The little love-god, lying once asleep,
Laid by his side his heart-inflaming brand,
Whilst many nymphs that vowed chaste life to keep,
Were deprived the victory they bravely earned.
Now, dead, stroying at his death not stained
By the remorse of redeemed remembrance,
The carnage of time he cast not into,
Feeds on and mounds life with vulgar trash.
Then, being in affectionate life,
Full oft his beautifying prize in stress,
Against friendly foes laid low his might,
Feeds on and is filled with minstrelsy.
  Like him, thou shalt be trodden down with pillage,
  And, being slain, thy beauty destroyed too.
