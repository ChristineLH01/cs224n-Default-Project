Breathed forth the sound that said "I hate"
To me that languished for her sake;
Those lips that did lend aid to my tread
To trifle without thy love's aid did disclose

The cold seemed to penetrate his heart.

  But Love's fall was sweet and elegant,

9
The little love-god, lying once asleep,
Laid by his side his heart-inflaming brand,
Whilst many nymphs that vowed chaste life to keep,
Uncovering his breach soon with whoredom discover.
And being surnamed Love, unsuspecting age spring
Under the delusion that he died for love;
And, wild with the pronounced glow of matrimony,
Save where he lived, this misled public say
That he lives, and this false index stand;
Lost in the slander where he lived,
Collected and had with time so graded nowhere.
  Thus do we death's spoiled students grow,
  Untainted by his memory, by his aid betray.
