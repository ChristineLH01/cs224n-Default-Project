When my love swears that she is made of truth,
I do believe her, though I know she lies,
That she might think me some untutored youth,
Who in my breast hath some unknown right.
However, much as I love her,
Even though I know she is untrue,

She holds me so, though I think otherwise,
That, yes, I am her false told,
And she hurts me with my belief,
