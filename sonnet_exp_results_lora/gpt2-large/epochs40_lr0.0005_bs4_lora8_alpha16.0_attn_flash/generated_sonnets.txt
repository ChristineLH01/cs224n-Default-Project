My love is as a fever, longing still
For that which longer nurseth the disease,
Feeding on that which doth preserve the ill,
Being weary of those that are past,
As eager for those that are turned away.
Love brings some happiness, though still living,
But dies not when absent, when forward
The infected part of his heart is severed,
O'erbalanced, not by feasting on health,
But by destroying his health, which his sweet liking
In glad parting puts off.
  Thus love is vain, whatever form it bear,
  But what for a cancer breeds songs of love.
