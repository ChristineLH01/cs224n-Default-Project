For that which longer nurseth the disease,

Love is too young to know what conscience is;
Yet who knows not conscience is born of love?
Then, gentle cheater, urge not my amiss,
Knowing yourself, yet repent not.
For I seek only what's mine, and myself wholly.
Besides, what claim is there on my dearest right,
If not my soul itself to be stamped there?
How dare I feel sorrow when my friend's duty
Requests what they owe to my weak intellect?
Worship your neighbour and yourself above;
Then neither this nor that my woes I care deem.

When my love swears that she is made of truth,
I do believe her, though I know she lies,
That she might think me some untutored youth,
Lying in deeper sap than philosophy has conquered.
Though weak from love's painful kiss, I cannot claim
That she loves me for a phase truly lent.
Her wicked right hands cause sin,
I know not why she loves me so much,
Though with gentle caresses it sounds both joy and pain.
No habit hates me there, still bent
To stretch my lover's chaste wings far and wide,
Unbent in admiration's sights and thought.
  Yet if she sees my folly, I shall be led
  To folly, for love has not gained mastery.
