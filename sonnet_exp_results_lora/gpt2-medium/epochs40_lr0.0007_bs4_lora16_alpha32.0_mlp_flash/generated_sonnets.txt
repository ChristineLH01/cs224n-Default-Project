Those lips that Love's own hand did make
Breathed forth the sound that said "I hate"
To me that languished for her sake;
But now my soul cherishes that sorrow, laments,
A melody of loss, sweet to hear,
Though the dryest wreath of perfume shall cover it.
Oh, bitter lesson of my disarray,
That taught me how to find the light,
To cherish dear the ties that tie me close,
And make each other's heart mourn, strengthen its link.
  For love's sweetest eyes, in sorrow they droop,
  And so, I too, cling to them, and worstow them light.

Love is too young to know what conscience is;
Yet who knows not conscience is born of love?
Then, gentle cheater, urge not my amiss,
Lest you become entwined in my transgressions.

Laid by his side his heart-inflaming brand,

Lose all, their souls nailed to the stake,
For love's sweet sacrifice shifts into infancy,
A banisher of vice, it forever pits.

Lost in lust's eternal night,
Lost to the darkness, forever in plight,
Her love, once valiant, now forsworn,
A shameful act, like all women, left forlorn,
Craving the very thing they should revere.

  Choosing what's most dearly they should strive.
