Two loves I have, of comfort and despair,
That like two spirits do suggest me still.
My better angel is a man right fair,
And his claim with me do seem slight;
He speaks with truth, my essence mute,
To mine as moving shadows are conveyed.
The spiritual one sees the affair
Of love disguised as phantom conceit;
The spiritual one pleases in falsehood
And hopes he shall clear the deception lying beneath.
I grant neither he nor I deceive with smiles;
He lies, and I neither knows nor knows well
How to spell his spirits nor his love's decay.
  Yet, in my error, he lies with his cost,
  And I am deceived by pride and am proved to have fallen.
