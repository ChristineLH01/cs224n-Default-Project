Those lips that Love's own hand did make
Breathed forth the sound that said "I hate"
To me that languished for her sake;
When yonder gaze falls in rage, flame's ire
Odded the colour thinner with wonder got on hold.
Or yesterday that honoured statue with sumptuous sight,
Made the tidings boast of sweet graces;
Ought any crownworthy art done this, "
That beauty's fair wear hid" from anyone.
Now washed away sorrows; hate uncovers fine lines
Or beauties the wrinkles of hair gleaming near.
  How there, wishing to shine brighter, would fury!
  So again blot out dreams, with clove in hand.

Poor soul, the center of my sinful earth,
Pressed with these rebel powers that thee array,
Why dost thou pine within and suffer dearth,
And send this matter against muddled bonds?
Am I not chastened with pride when thou makes most virtue of my pique?
Whatever actions thou take before,
That thou pull yet trifling justice amongst broken hearts,
Why me by thine own judicious views lend
No factional character when judicious eyes conceive
The world of thieves, and chieftains ensnared with eye,
Seasined upon them, devouring innocence born anew.
  Worship my ignorance with an both obscene and flawed charm,
  By planting folly within thy heart's cunning art.

O me, what eyes hath love put in my head,
Which have no correspondence with true sight!
Or if they have, where is my judgment fled,
Dly charity fed by deception?
Or if my beasts were ever present when,
Can man can attribute their powers to say,
My soul to be omitting true love's stirring grace?
Since most of my body says my name,
I lie, separated from friends, and bidden,
For custom has misunderstood a gift from my haughty beauty;
Had social lovers rejoiced that my lover might possess
A son, and love herself cleaned, sweetly pleased.
  In truth, my friend's eye so interprets,
  That all faults come my way from our sight.

O, from what power hast thou this powerful might
With insufficiency my heart to sway?
To make me give the lie to my true sight,
By boldly concealing words, easy whispers,
Misuse principles throughout my tail.
Who knew the secrets of tongues on earth
O'er deep or deep within cells, which send word,
When wit slumbers hidden in secret ropes?
My conscience, now weary of speaking to friends,
Thou'd least witness to my words, wholly polluted;
Thus, came the arrival of words, made my eyes busy,
Trapped by repeated speech I spent in ages waste.
  For men's faculties prove their deepest awesomeness;
  Think better when praise produces truth advanced.

When my love swears that she is made of truth,
I do believe her, though I know she lies,
That she might think me some untutored youth, proper,
Yet a swell of knowing bonds unwhipped by mist.
The whirl of desire ensues me in delusion,
And I converse as if by desire roams:
We split asunder; stars are cornered
In opposite sides, by wrongs heaved.
Thy tripartite testicle finds me full wet,
Consumed with love's obsessive pleading;
My heart, full of shame, goes wild,
Avolaging, losing sense of where she lies.
  This truth has passed, and I am convinced.
