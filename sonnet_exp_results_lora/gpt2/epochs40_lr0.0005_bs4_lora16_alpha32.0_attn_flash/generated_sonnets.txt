Poor soul, the center of my sinful earth,
Pressed with these rebel powers that thee array,
Why dost thou pine within and suffer dearth,
Like a hurricane pulling more mire down doth doth call?
Nor does thy priceless world be abused,
For all sleep doth give form to fury,
And call thee poor nimblest & mute muse
Where her sweetest moments dance and delicate quick;
And though thy prettiest voice seemed calmer than sleep's blood,
Sleep's own away shall keep thee and me up.
  Still I think of thee, thy sweetness after death,
  Which sorrow awakens in me without a strong end.
