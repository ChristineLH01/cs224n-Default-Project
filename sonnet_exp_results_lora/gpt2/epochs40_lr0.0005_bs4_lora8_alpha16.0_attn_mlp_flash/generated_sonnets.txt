Breathed forth the sound that said "I hate"
To me that languished for her sake;
With desire's trace, with eyes, thought and deed,
Her heart, with pitch, converted by love's pleas.

She holds the candle to her joy,
Which stirs eternal day, seeking the light of day,
Curing through darkness till night's pall.

Poor soul, the center of my sinful earth,
Pressed with these rebel powers that thee array,
Why dost thou pine within and suffer dearth,
Despairting thy dusky light with hideous tint?
Why so oft does my sensual pride defend
Its talismanry against that foe whom thy will?
For in thy hue, I am less bound
To sin than by thy deeds, more bad still;
Thus, thine watch and mine, it abides,
By actions that thy unworthy rage doth thwart.
  For this reason, I am guilty, and myself is lost,
  For my sake of thee, I dance with thee, as thunder flies.

Love is too young to know what conscience is;
Yet who knows not conscience is born of love?
Then, gentle cheater, urge not my amiss,
Love for love that wavers in summer's glare;
