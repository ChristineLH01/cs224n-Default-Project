Poor soul, the center of my sinful earth,
Pressed with these rebel powers that thee array,
Why dost thou pine within and suffer dearth,
While thy outer shell doth crumble and crumble?
To whom dost thou send such great clouds,
Shall thou confound the very most rich in parting,
And leave no legacy but despair?
Shall thy decay be my fault, if thou continue
To starve the world, and all its water?
'Tis not thy will, though plentiful life brings
Returning th' misgiving, to let oblivion rip.
  Use them thou art to strain the golem's grasp,
  And they shall profit thee in their waterless sport.

My love is as a fever, longing still
For that which longer nurseth the disease,
Feeding on that which doth preserve the ill,

Thus, I watch the day through fathomless depths,
That love's sweet veil may reveal,

  Or nought is that love, which doth latch on in vain.

That like two spirits do suggest me still.
