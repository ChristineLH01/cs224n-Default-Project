Poor soul, the center of my sinful earth,
Pressed with these rebel powers that thee array,
Why dost thou pine within and suffer dearth,
The artful execution of a destitute heart?
Your iron rod won't expose thy waste,
Nor will it this remote remedy sentence,
Therefore depart, forsaking all it clung.
Who dare such a gracious host grant such sanction,
For unto this teaching lay my sorrow,
The remedy, and free remedy, shall endure
By my lawful senses, granted grace and truth.
  Such gentle contemplation, when rendered with zest,
  Shall brighten thy day, and dull thy morning so.

My love is as a fever, longing still
For that which longer nurseth the disease,

Yet who knows not conscience is born of love?
Then, gentle cheater, urge not my amiss,
Lest age deceive my soul, and you prove

Two loves I have, of comfort and despair,
That like two spirits do suggest me still.
My better angel is a man right fair,
Love which shows desire within my heart.
Thy being in love belongs to heaven's divide,
Divided is not to depart until my heart there.
This it proves very vague, my vow and my need,
And usually hidden bind me to it's castle's command.
My love is mild when strong as charcoal,
Perhaps sweetest society sends clear;
Love possesses it power, though weak so.
  To love is true love outlived, in love is false.
