Poor soul, the center of my sinful earth,
Pressed with these rebel powers that thee array,
Why dost thou pine within and suffer dearth,
The foundations of a wily, immature self?
None but my eye, destined to shepherd thee at this peak,
And bidding it to scrawl, makes thee fall,
Stealing thy vital resource, a consummate fail.
Upon this impersonal voice, I bend,
A legislative wish, granting thee grace,
Yet bearing stern blows to my conscience,
The villain seeking revenge, and sending
A gentle sigh, 'gainst thou my soul.
  My heart, now defiled, shall find rest,
