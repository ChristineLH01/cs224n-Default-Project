Poor soul, the center of my sinful earth,
Pressed with these rebel powers that thee array,
Why dost thou pine within and suffer dearth,
Thy license such a cherished rod to spy?
No augury nor crown groweth me naught any grove,

Feeling well-timed even with daylight;

To corrupt thy belly.
  A plague smolders through many seasons here,
